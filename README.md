n behalf of the board of directors, doctors, and staff of Dhanalakshmi Hospital, I have great pleasure to welcome you to our website.

It was the dream of our beloved father, the late Sri C.M. Ramunni , founder of Dhanalakshmi Group of companies. He was a highly respected pioneering handloom textile industrialist and philanthropist of Cannanore who realized the importance of modern healthcare in the backward region of North Malabar. When two of his sons qualified as doctors this dream even became bigger. A large plot of land was procured in the heart of Cannanore town where the hospital was intended to be built. However his dreams bore fruit only after his demise when his sons took up the mission, got the hospital constructed and dedicated it in his memory. The first phase of the hospital building was completed in mid 1998 and handed over to the newly formed company, Dhanalakshmi Hospitals Pvt. Ltd. The official inauguration of the hospital was on 30th October 1998. To start with we had only 50 beds which soon was increased to 100 beds and planned to have 350 bed capacities within three years.

Dhanalakshmi Hospital now has outpatient and inpatient facilities to cater to all economic sections of patients. We have fully automated modern clinical laboratories, Imaging services with state of the art equipments, Well equipped Coronary Care unit, medical pediatric and surgical ICU's , Emergency Medicine Services, Modern and well equipped Operation theaters and labor rooms besides a level 3 NICU and step-down ICU with highly trained staff and a modern Renal dialysis unit. The hospital has its own Blood storage facilities and has separate inpatient and outpatient pharmacies with 24 hours service. The emergency department runs round the clock and is supported with reliable ambulance services.

Besides the existing general specialties, several more specialties and super specialties were added as more and more highly qualified specialist doctors joined our panel of doctors. Within a short period the hospital has started to make a name for itself. The management soon realized the importance of 'quality health care' and started the process of getting the hospital National Accreditation. In March 2014 after a sincere work and effort, Dhanalakshmi Hospital secured the prestigious NABH Accreditation, and became the 12th hospital in Kerala and the only one in North Malabar to be nationally accredited. With NABH Accreditation Dhanalakshmi Hospiital ensures our patients quality health care with priority to patient safety at all times. Established treatment protocols and policies form the basis of the service provided by our panel of highly competent and qualified doctors, nursing and paramedical staff. NABH Accreditation also empowers our patients and continuous quality improvement is ensured.

Affiliated to Dhanalakshmi Hospital are our two Nursing Institutions, Dhanalakshmi College of Nursing affiliated to the Karala Health University and Dhanalakshmi School of Nursing, affiliated to Kerala Nursing council. Both courses have the recognition of the Kerala Nursing Council and the Indian Nursing Council. Dhanalakshmi College of Nursing conducts two degree courses : the 4 year B.Sc. Nursing and the condensed 2 year Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing course (for GNM candidates) and the School of Nursing conducts the 3 Year GNM Diploma course. Hundreds of Nurses have qualified from our two institutions so far. The Nursing Institutions have always maintained excellent examination results which itself speaks highly of the excellent teaching and training being imparted by experienced and well qualified teaching faculty.

The hospital with its modern building and excellent infrastructure is situated in the heart of the city, yet it is easily accessible. The premises are abundant in greenery and a lot of importance is given to the protection of the environment. We strive to make our patients stay in the hospital a pleasant and comfortable one and have given utmost importance in providing quality healthcare.

I wish all our patients visiting us 'Good Health'!

Employee satisfaction is the measure of how content, motivated, and fulfilled employees feel in their roles, influenced by factors like compensation, work environment, and leadership. It is crucial for high retention and productivity. A positive, supportive workplace culture, clear communication, and opportunities for career growth are essential to creating a satisfying environment.

KEY COMPONENT'S DRIVE'S OF EMPLOYEES SATISFACTION IN DHANALAKSHMI HOSPITAL

<img width="768" height="549" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6cde1590-8f78-4ca2-8020-19513bbc2564" />











Intrinsic & Extrinsic Factors: Satisfaction stems from both internal fulfillment (meaningful work, autonomy) and external rewards (salary, benefits, job security).
Work Environment & Culture: A, supportive, and safe working environment is fundamental to employee happiness.
Leadership and Management: Consistent, transparent leadership that recognizes contributions helps employees feel valued.
Career Growth: Employees feel more satisfied when they have opportunities to develop skills and advance in their roles.
Relationships: Positive relationships and teamwork with colleagues improve job satisfaction. 

IDENTIFICATION KEY DRIVES OF EMPLOYEES SATISFACTION IN DHANALAKSHMI HOSPITAL

<img width="1350" height="759" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e262da0a-013f-4806-b5aa-3a3e24831153" />









Drivers Beyond Pay: While competitive compensation is foundational, it is no longer the sole driver of satisfaction. Key drivers include recognition, work-life balance, career development, and workplace relationships.
Importance of Recognition: Employees who do not feel adequately recognized are twice as likely to quit within a year.
Work-Life Balance is Top Priority: Following the pandemic, 83% of workers surveyed ranked work-life balance as a top priority, often ranking ahead of salary.
The Impact of Management: Employees often leave managers, not companies. Supportive management and the absence of micromanagement are crucial for 
satisfaction.
Connection to Purpose: Employees are more satisfied when they understand how their daily work contributes to the broader company mission.
Social Connections: Work friendships can increase job satisfaction by up to 50.

SUGGESHION'S TO IMPROVE EMPLOYEES SATISFACTION IN DHANALAKSHMI HOSPITAL

<img width="3040" height="892" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5704c6f1-002c-4cd9-829b-7acf80d67ba4" />









Implement Regular Recognition Programs: Move beyond annual reviews to provide frequent, specific, and public recognition for achievements.
Foster a Culture of Transparency: Keep employees in the loop regarding company decisions and strategies to build trust.
Offer Flexible Work Arrangements: Provide options like hybrid work, remote work, or flexible hours to help employees manage their personal lives.
Invest in Career Development: Offer clear paths for growth, such as mentoring, training programs, and skill-building workshops.
Equip Managers to Lead: Train managers to focus on coaching and support rather than micromanagement.
Regularly Measure with Surveys: Use anonymous, frequent "pulse" surveys to take the temperature of the organization and act on feedback quickly.
Improve Workplace Environment: Ensure physical and digital tools are user-friendly, and the workspace is comfortable.

1.Compensation and Benefits
Salary and Incentives: A fair salary and incentives (such as overtime pay, bonuses) are major drivers.
Job Security: A stable, secure job is a key factor, particularly in private healthcare settings.
2. Work Environment and Conditions
Workload Management: Reasonable work hours and manageable patient-to-staff ratios are crucial. High workloads frequently cause dissatisfaction.
Physical Environment: Clean, safe, and well-equipped surroundings.
Working Conditions: The availability of necessary materials and equipment to perform duties efficiently.
3.Organizational Factors
Leadership and Management Support: Supportive leadership that provides feedback and recognition is essential.
Career Advancement: Opportunities for professional development, training, and promotion.
Organizational Culture: A collaborative, respectful culture that promotes teamwork and support from peers.
4. Interpersonal Relationships
Supervisor Support: A positive relationship with immediate supervisors.
Colleague Cooperation: Good, supportive relationships with coworkers.
5. Individual Factors
Work-Life Balance: The ability to balance personal and professional life.
Autonomy: The opportunity to use one’s skills and have input into decisions.
